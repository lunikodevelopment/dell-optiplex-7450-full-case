# V3.1 hardware BOM

Structural interface:
- 4 × M4 Dell VESA screws — usable thread depth / final screw length remains unit-specific
- 4 × M4 fender washers, about 20 mm OD
- 4 × M8 steel carriage bolts / equivalent studs
- 4 × M8 large washers
- 4 × M8 locking nuts / thin jam nuts as required by the actual stud-head stack
- 1 × M5 socket-head screw for the positive anti-lift block
- 1 × M5 square nut for the receiver pocket
- optional 2 mm aluminum receiver backer
- optional secondary steel tether

Enclosure:
- M3 screws, washers and nyloc nuts for rear seam joiners
- 8 × M3 threaded inserts (or service-selected equivalent) for front retainer tabs
- 8 × M3 front-retainer screws

Wall:
- fasteners/anchors selected for the actual wall substrate; the CAD defines hole layout, not substrate-specific anchor selection.
