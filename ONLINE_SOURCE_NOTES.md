# Online source notes — V3.1

Sources used to establish the design basis and cross-check the clearance-first revision:

1. **Dell OptiPlex 7450 AIO Owner's Manual — VESA wall mount**: confirms 100 × 100 mm VESA, M4 screws and a minimum 20 mm wall clearance.
2. **Dell OptiPlex 7450 AIO Owner's Manual — physical specifications**: confirms 575.24 mm width, 392.90 mm height, 63.5 mm non-touch depth / 62.79 mm touch depth.
3. **Dell OptiPlex 7450 AIO visual guide / service documentation**: confirms stand removal and the general external I/O, control and ventilation regions.
4. **Independent used-system rear photographs**: corroborate the square recessed stand bay and bottom-center latch arrangement after the stand is removed.
5. **Genuine Dell 7450VESA replacement-bracket photographs**: corroborate the internal VESA bracket arrangement.
6. **Bambu Lab H2C product documentation**: used only as an external printer-size cross-check; V3.1 nevertheless constrains its H2C parts to a conservative 300 × 315 × 315 mm design envelope.

No online source located during the V3.1 research provided a dimensioned engineering drawing of the exterior stand recess, exact rear curvature, or usable M4 thread depth. The receiver was therefore reduced to 128 × 128 mm, given an open-bottom latch relief, and combined with larger enclosure clearances so those unpublished dimensions are not required for nominal fit.

The photo-derived rear-bay figures in the README and revalidation report use the independently known 100 mm VESA spacing as an image scale. They are corroborative estimates rather than factory tolerances.
